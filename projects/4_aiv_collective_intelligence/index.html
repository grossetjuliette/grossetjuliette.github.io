Collective intelligence for autonomous industrial vehicles

Juliette Grosset, Alain-Jérôme Fougères, Moïse Djoko-Kouam, Jean-Marie Bonnin

IMT Atlantique · ECAM Rennes – Louis de Broglie · IRISA (EASE team)

Thesis (HAL) Defence video Thesis prize AIV Simulator CARLA–V2X architecture

Overview

My PhD, conducted at IMT Atlantique and ECAM Rennes – Louis de Broglie within the EASE team at IRISA, focused on collective intelligence strategies for efficient autonomous industrial vehicles (AIVs) in Industry 4.0 contexts. Co-funded by Région Bretagne (ARED) and ECAM, the thesis received the Best Thesis Award of the SPIN Doctoral School (2025).

Manuscript — HAL tel-04921399

Contributions

The manuscript is organised in five chapters. Each contribution chapter is linked below to the publications it cites as its basis.

1

State of the art

Autonomous industrial vehicles in Industry 4.0, cooperative ITS and V2X communication, agent-based simulation, obstacle avoidance, task allocation and energy management.

2

Collective Obstacle Avoidance Strategies

A collective strategy added to an intersection collision-avoidance algorithm, a global obstacle-avoidance strategy based on shared perception, the adaptation of standardised V2X messages (CAM, DENM, CPM, MCM) to Industry 4.0, and the preparation of real experiments with ROS 2 robots.

ARCI 2022 ASPAI 2022 Integrated Computer-Aided Engineering 2023 (Q1) Book chapter 2024

3

Collective Task Allocation Strategies

A dynamic task (re)allocation strategy in V2X cooperation mode with the infrastructure, evaluated in multi-agent simulation, and the impact of trust in exchanged messages on allocation.

Integrated Computer-Aided Engineering 2024 (Q1)

4

Cooperative Perception Strategies

An architecture coupling the CARLA simulator, ROS 2 and OpenCDA to generate standardised V2X messages for cooperative perception, applied to pedestrian safety — developed during the research visit at the University of Tokyo.

VTC2024-Fall

5

Collective Energy Management Strategies

A fuzzy decision model for battery recharging, fuzzy agent-based simulation of fleet recharging in an airport baggage-handling scenario, and the joint problem of recharging and task allocation.

ASPAI 2024 — Best Paper AISyS 2024 Algorithms 2024 (Q2)

AIV Simulator
