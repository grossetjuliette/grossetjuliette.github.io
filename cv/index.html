Experience

  • 2025 – present

    Adelaide, Australia

    Post-Doctoral Researcher
    Naval Group / IRL CROSSING, CNRS
    Maritime simulation, multi-agent systems and autonomous robotics in multi-domain environments (aerial, surface, underwater).
    • Development of LOTUSim, an open-source real-time maritime simulation platform for human-vehicle teaming, and of LOTUSim-Energy, its energy-oriented extension developed within the FACET project.
    • Real-time physics-based ocean current simulation and multi-layer underwater current modelling for maritime robotics.
    • Benchmarking of Gazebo-based and alternative maritime simulation environments for swarm robotics and AI training.
    • Member of the LOTUSim Technical Committee and co-leader of the Maritime Working Group at IRL CROSSING; in 2026, Naval Group representative on the IRL CROSSING Management Committee, and elected as a non-permanent member of the IRL CROSSING Laboratory Council.
