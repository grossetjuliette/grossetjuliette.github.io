Juliette Grosset

Post-Doctoral Researcher · IRL CROSSING (CNRS) / Naval Group · Adelaide, Australia

Since February 2025, I am a Post-Doctoral Researcher at Naval Group, hosted in the IRL CROSSING, CNRS laboratory in Adelaide, Australia. My research focuses on maritime simulation, multi-agent systems and autonomous robotics in multi-domain environments (aerial, surface and underwater). A central part of my work is LOTUSim, an open-source real-time maritime simulation platform for human-vehicle teaming, and LOTUSim-Energy, its offshore-energy extension developed within the FACET project (French-Australian Government funding). Contributions include real-time physics-based multi-layer ocean current modelling and benchmarking of maritime simulators for swarm robotics and AI training. I led the open-source release of LOTUSim (November 2025), the first simulator ever open-sourced by Naval Group.

My PhD, conducted at IMT Atlantique and ECAM Rennes – Louis de Broglie within the EASE team at IRISA, focused on collective intelligence strategies for efficient autonomous industrial vehicles (AIVs) in Industry 4.0 contexts: a shared-perception-based obstacle avoidance strategy, the adaptation of V2X communication standards to Industry 4.0, a decentralised dynamic task (re)allocation mechanism, a V2X data generation architecture, and fuzzy-model-based energy management strategies. Co-funded by Région Bretagne (ARED) and ECAM, the thesis received the Best Thesis Award of the SPIN Doctoral School (2025). I hold the CNU 27 qualification (Computer Science).

I am a member of the LOTUSim Technical Committee, co-lead the Maritime Working Group at IRL CROSSING, served in 2026 as Naval Group representative on its Management Committee, and was elected non-permanent member of its Laboratory Council (August 2026).

Selected publications

  1. IROS-W
    LOTUSim-Energy: A Maritime Simulator for Human-Drone Interaction in Autonomous Offshore Operation & Maintenance
    Juliette Grosset, Marie Dubromel, Hélène Lechêne, and 2 more authors
    In IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS) – Workshop AQ²UASIM-v2, Sep 2026
    Abs Bib HTML Code Website

    LOTUSim-Energy is a real-time maritime simulator for multi-domain human-drone interaction in offshore wind operation and maintenance. It unifies heterogeneous unmanned vehicles (UAVs, USVs, AUVs, ROVs) within a distributed architecture coupling environmental forcing (wind, waves, currents), immersive desktop/VR supervision, energy-aware battery modeling, and fault-detection pipelines, demonstrated on a multi-domain inspection scenario for offshore wind turbine monopile and transition-piece structures.

  2. IROS
    LOTUSim: Multi-Domain Simulator for Marine Robotics
    Cédric Buche, Juliette Grosset, Hélène Lechêne, and 4 more authors
    In IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS), Sep 2026
    Abs arXiv Bib HTML Code

    Simulation is essential for maritime robotics, supporting operator training, mission rehearsal, and human-vehicle interaction in environments where real-world testing is costly or hazardous. LOTUSim is an open-source, real-time maritime simulator supporting multi-user interaction across aerial, surface, and underwater robotic systems for coordinated naval-style operations, combining real-time interactive performance at scale with an Ekman-inspired underwater current model validated against ocean reanalysis data.
